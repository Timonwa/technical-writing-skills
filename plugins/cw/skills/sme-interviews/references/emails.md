# Interview emails

Fill every `<…>`; keep each email short enough to read without scrolling. The guide's voice and sign-off apply.

## Request

```text
Subject: Interview request: <the piece, in a few words>

Hi <name>,

I'm writing <the piece> for <the publication>, read by <who reads it>. It's about <the topic>, and I'd value your view on <the specific thing they know>.

Could we talk for <20–30> minutes, by <call · video · in person>, before <date>? If email suits you better, I can send <five to seven> questions instead.

How it would work:
- It's on the record: you'd be quoted by name as <name, role, organisation>, unless we agree otherwise first.
- With your consent, I'd record it so your words are quoted exactly.
- <Our policy on seeing quotes or facts before publication.>
- It's due to run around <date>.

<Sign-off>
```

## Email interview

```text
Subject: Questions for <the piece>

Hi <name>,

Thank you for agreeing to answer by email. The ground rules are as we agreed: <on the record, quoted as …>. Could you reply by <date>?

1. <Question, answerable on its own.>
2. <Question.>
3. <Question.>

If there's something I haven't asked that I should have, please add it.

<Sign-off>
```

## Check-back

Facts, not wording: a figure, a date, a name's spelling, a technical claim.

```text
Subject: Checking a few facts: <the piece>

Hi <name>,

Thank you again for your time. Before <the piece> runs on <date>, could you confirm these facts by <date>?

1. <Fact, as the piece states it.>
2. <Fact.>

You'll be identified as <name, role, organisation>. <What they may review, under the publication's policy.>

<Sign-off>
```
