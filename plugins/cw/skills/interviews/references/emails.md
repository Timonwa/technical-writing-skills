# Interview emails

Fill every `<…>`; keep each email short enough to read without scrolling. The guide's voice and sign-off apply.

## Request

```text
Subject: Interview request: <the piece, in a few words>

Hi <name>,

I'm writing <the piece — an article, a Q&A, a profile, a case study> for <the publication>, read by <who reads it>. It's about <the topic>, and I'd like to hear <what you want from them — your view on…, your experience of…, the story of…>.

Could we talk for <20–60> minutes, by <call · video · in person>, before <date>? If email suits you better, I can send <five to seven> questions instead.

How it would work:
- It's on the record: you'd be named as <name, role, organisation, or how they're connected>, unless we agree otherwise first.
- With your consent, I'd record it so your words are quoted exactly.
- <What they'll see before publication: facts to check, under our policy · the edited Q&A, case study or testimonial, to approve before it runs.>
- It's due to run around <date>.

<Sign-off>
```

## Email interview

```text
Subject: Questions for <the piece>

Hi <name>,

Thank you for agreeing to answer by email. The ground rules are as we agreed: <on the record, named as …>. Could you reply by <date>?

1. <Question, answerable on its own.>
2. <Question.>
3. <Question.>

If there's something I haven't asked that I should have, please add it.

<Sign-off>
```

## Check-back

Facts, not wording: a figure, a date, a name's spelling.

```text
Subject: Checking a few facts: <the piece>

Hi <name>,

Thank you again for your time. Before <the piece> runs on <date>, could you confirm these facts by <date>?

1. <Fact, as the piece states it.>
2. <Fact.>

You'll be identified as <name, role, organisation, or how they're connected>. <What they may review, under the publication's policy.>

<Sign-off>
```

## Approval

For a Q&A article, a case study or a testimonial: the version that will run, sent whole.

```text
Subject: For your approval: <the piece>

Hi <name>,

Thank you again for your time. Below is <the edited Q&A · the case study · your testimonial> as it will run on <date>, <where>.

<For a Q&A: It's edited for length and clarity; please tell me if anything no longer says what you meant.>
<For a case study: Please check every number against your own records, and confirm <the business> is happy to be named.>
<For a testimonial: These are your words, and nothing will run that you haven't approved.>

Could you reply by <date> to say it's approved, or with any corrections?

<The full text.>

<Sign-off>
```
