# Q&A article

The edited interview, published as the piece. It goes in the format file the brief lists — usually `blog.md`, after the front matter — and the notes file in `interviews/` keeps the full transcript and the consent. Copy the block below. Italic text is a hint; replace it. Keep every part, and leave one blank rather than removing it until the writer decides.

```markdown
_Intro, one or two short paragraphs: who they are, in the terms the reader needs — "Funmi Adeyemi has run a two-oven bakery in Ibadan since 2018" — why their answers matter to this reader now, and one detail that shows the person rather than describes them._

_How the interview happened: "We spoke by phone in March."_

**_Question 1 — as asked, or tightened without changing what was asked_**

_Answer, in their words: trimmed for length and clarity, never reworded to say something they didn't._

**_Question 2_**

_Answer._

**_Question 3_**

_Answer._

**_Question 4_**

_Answer._

**_Question 5_**

_Answer._

_Close: their last answer, or one line on where to find them or their work._

_This interview has been edited for length and clarity._
```

## Editing the answers

- **May change** (common practice): filler, false starts and repetition; a long answer trimmed to its point; questions put in an order that reads well, each with its own answer.
- **Never changes** (common practice): what they meant; a word inside an answer to make it say something they didn't; an answer moved under a question it wasn't given to; answers to different questions merged into one.
- **The questions are the writer's**, so they may be tightened, but a question that shaped the answer — a leading one, a correction — stays as asked.
- **Check every answer against the audio**, as for any quote; a line that can't be checked is marked for the writer, not published.

## Sign-off

Send the edited version whole with the [approval email](emails.md#approval) and record the person's consent to publish it in the notes file's consent table (common practice). A correction of fact or a misheard word is made; a change that alters what they said on the record goes to the writer to decide.
