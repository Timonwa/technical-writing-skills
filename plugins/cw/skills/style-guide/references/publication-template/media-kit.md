# Media kit — <publication name>

**What you send sponsors — your audience, numbers, slots and prices.** Who the publication is for is [profile.md](profile.md); how it looks is [brand.md](brand.md), never repeated here.

Fill in what you know. Leave the rest as `unset` and a skill will ask rather than guess. Every number is read from the platform or analytics tool and dated; none is estimated, rounded up or carried over without a new date. `cw:pitching` asks for current figures before it sends this to a sponsor.
