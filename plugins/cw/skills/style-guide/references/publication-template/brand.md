## Colours

One row per colour, with its role. Four or five colours usually cover a publication.

| Name | Hex | Role                                               | Use with |
| ---- | --- | -------------------------------------------------- | -------- |
|      |     | _text · background · accent · link · chart series_ |          |

**Contrast.** Text on a background needs at least 4.5:1, or 3:1 for large text — 18 pt, or 14 pt bold ([WCAG 1.4.3](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/contrast-minimum.html)). The parts of a chart or diagram a reader needs to understand it need 3:1 against what's next to them ([WCAG 1.4.11](https://www.w3.org/WAI/WCAG22/Understanding/non-text-contrast.html)). List the pairs that pass in "Use with"; text inside the logo itself is exempt.
