# Calendar — <publication name>

**The editorial calendar: one line per topic.** Everything else about a topic lives in its own folder under `topics/`; the dates for each format it becomes are in its `brief.md`.

Change only your own topic's line, and never rewrite the file: someone else may be working on another topic. Each line reads: first publish date · topic folder · formats · status · held by · series (left out when there's none) · link.

Status: `idea` · `briefed` · `drafting` · `in review` · `scheduled` · `published` · `paused` · `retired`.

A topic's line sits in the section that matches it, and moves when that changes: a topic with a publish date goes under **Coming up**, soonest first; one without a date under **Ideas**, newest first; a published or retired topic under **Published**, newest first. An idea line has status `idea`, where it came from and its score, and no folder until it's briefed.

**Example lines** — the shape of each, not real entries:

- Under Coming up: 2026-12-04 · 2026-12-sourdough-starter · blog, newsletter, social · briefed · — · series: bread-basics (1) · [topics/2026-12-sourdough-starter](topics/2026-12-sourdough-starter/)
- Under Ideas: — · keeping a sourdough starter alive while travelling · idea · from: a reader's reply · score: fit 3, value 3, demand 2, experience 3, effort 1, return 2
