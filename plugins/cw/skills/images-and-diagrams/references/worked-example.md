# Worked example: the visuals for one piece

A personal-finance publication is running "What I learned tracking every naira for a year" — a first-person piece with one chart, two photos and a newsletter version. Here's how each visual is decided, credited and described.

## The draft's visuals

1. A stock photo of coins on a table, as the header.
2. A photo of the writer's handwritten spending notebook.
3. A bar chart of monthly spending, its y-axis starting at ₦80,000.
4. A screenshot of the budgeting app's dashboard, with the writer's real account number visible.
5. An animated GIF of the app's category sorting, for the newsletter.

## What happens to each

| Visual        | Decision                                    | Why                                                                                                                |
| ------------- | ------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Stock coins   | Cut, or replaced with a real photo          | Generic stock is decoration; readers skip it. The header slot is the cover image, which is `cw:cover-images`' job. |
| The notebook  | Keep — it's the evidence the piece rests on | The real thing is content. The writer owns it; no consent needed beyond their own.                                 |
| The bar chart | Redraw from zero; title states the finding  | A y-axis from ₦80,000 makes a 10% change look like a doubling.                                                     |
| The dashboard | Recapture with demo data                    | A real account number is personal data.                                                                            |
| The GIF       | Keep, with the key frame first              | Some Outlook versions show only the first frame.                                                                   |

## The chart, before and after

Before — title "Monthly spending, 2025"; y-axis ₦80,000–₦120,000; a legend for "Spending" and "Budget"; no source.

After:

- **Title states the finding:** "I spent less than my budget in nine months of twelve".
- **Axis from zero**, so the bars' lengths are honest.
- **Lines labelled directly** — "Budget" written at the end of its line, no legend.
- **Two colours that differ in lightness**, not red against green, so a colour-blind reader can tell them apart.
- **Caption:** "Source: the writer's own records, January–December 2025."
- **Alt text:** "Bar chart: monthly spending stayed under budget in nine of twelve months, and went over in March, August and December." The monthly figures sit in a table under the chart.

## Alt text for the notebook

Not "a notebook with writing" — the alt says what the reader needs from it here: "A page of the spending notebook: each day's purchases listed by hand, with a running total in the margin."

## Credits

The notebook photo is the writer's own — no credit line needed under the guide's policy. Had the coins image stayed, it would have needed its licence followed and, for a Creative Commons image, a full credit: title, author, source and licence.
