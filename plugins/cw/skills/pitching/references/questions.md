# Ask first — the questions for each kind

Ask the kind first, then the questions for that kind. Skip any question the request, `profile.md`, `media-kit.md`, the partner's guidelines, `pitch-log.md` or the topic's `brief.md` already answers; where one of them has an answer, show it and ask whether to use it.

**Which kind of collaboration is it?** writing for them · swap · co-written · guest on ours · sponsored.

## Writing for them

A guest post, a paid technical writing program or a commission on another publication.

1. **Which outlet, and where are its contributor guidelines?** Short answer: the outlet and a link.
2. **What does writing for it serve?** audience · portfolio · income. Default: the goals in `profile.md`.
3. **What's the idea, in one or two sentences?** Short answer: what the reader will be able to do or understand.
4. **What first-hand experience or data do you bring to it?** Short answer.
5. **Why now?** a release · a change · a common question · no particular reason.
6. **What length, and when can you deliver?** Short answer: words and a date.
7. **Which two or three published pieces show you can write it?** Links.
8. **Has this idea gone to another outlet?** no · yes, still open (which) · yes, declined.

## Swap

You write one for their blog or newsletter; they write one for yours.

1. **Who's the partner, and where do they publish?** Short answer: name, blog or newsletter, link.
2. **Why do their readers and yours fit each other?** Short answer: the overlap.
3. **What will you write for them, and what would you like from them?** Short answer: a topic each.
4. **Which formats?** blog post · newsletter issue · both, for each side.
5. **When does each piece go out?** Two dates, or "same week".
6. **What does each side give back?** a link to the author's site · a bio and headshot · a mention in social posts · a newsletter mention.
7. **Do you have contributor guidelines to send them?** yes (link) · no, write them.
8. **What are their house rules for your piece?** Short answer: length, format, links, topics they take — or their guidelines' link.

## Co-written

One piece written together.

1. **Who's the co-author, and what do they bring?** Short answer.
2. **What's the piece, in one or two sentences?** Short answer.
3. **Who writes which part?** Short answer: sections or roles (one drafts, one edits).
4. **Whose platform publishes it first?** yours · theirs.
5. **Does the other platform take a full copy or an opening excerpt, and when?** full copy · opening excerpt · neither; a date.
6. **How do the bylines read?** both names, in which order · one name with "with".
7. **Whose writing guide does it follow?** the first publisher's · agreed per point.

## Guest on ours

Someone pitched you, or you're inviting them.

1. **Did they pitch you, or are you inviting them?** they pitched · inviting.
2. **Who are they, and what's the pitch or the topic you want?** Short answer and a link.
3. **Does it fit your readers and what you've already published?** yes · with changes (which) · no.
4. **Your answer?** accept · ask to revise · decline.
5. **Is it paid, and what rights do you ask for?** unpaid · a fee (amount); first publication · exclusive for a period · non-exclusive.
6. **When's the draft due, and how many rounds of edits?** A date and a number.
7. **Do you have contributor guidelines to send?** yes (link) · no, write them.

## Sponsored

A brand pays to appear on your blog or newsletter.

1. **Are you pitching the brand, or did they approach you?** pitching · they approached.
2. **Who's the brand, and why does it fit your readers?** Short answer: brand, product, the reader who'd want it.
3. **What are they buying?** a slot in an issue · a whole issue · a sponsored post.
4. **What are your current numbers, and the date you read them?** subscribers · open rate · click rate · monthly readers. Default: `media-kit.md`, if its dates are recent.
5. **What's the price and the dates?** Default: the rate card in `media-kit.md`.
6. **What won't you say or do for any sponsor?** Default: "What it will never do" in `profile.md`.
7. **Which markets do your readers live in?** Short answer: the countries, which decide the disclosure rules that apply.
8. **Who approves the copy, and in how many rounds?** you alone · the sponsor reviews for facts · the sponsor approves (a number of rounds).
