# Ask first — the questions for each kind

Ask the kind first, then the questions for that kind. Skip any question the request, `profile.md`, `media-kit.md`, the partner's guidelines, `pitch-log.md`, the topic's `brief.md` or the email thread already answers; where one of them has an answer, show it and ask whether to use it.

**Which kind is it?** writing for them · swap · co-written · guest on ours · sponsored.

## Writing for them

A guest post or a commission for another publication.

1. **Which outlet, who's the editor, and where are its contributor guidelines?** Short answer: the outlet, a name and a link.
2. **Who reads it, and what has it run on this lately?** Short answer, from its guidelines and its last ten or so pieces.
3. **What do its guidelines ask a pitch to include?** an outline · a draft · clips · a word or character cap · a form — or the guidelines' link.
4. **What's the idea and the angle?** Short answer: what the reader will be able to do or understand, and how it differs from what they've run.
5. **Why you, and why now?** Short answer: first-hand experience or data; a release, a change, a common question, or no particular reason.
6. **What length, and when can you deliver?** Short answer: words and a date.
7. **Which two or three published pieces show you can write it?** Links.
8. **Has this idea gone to another outlet?** no · yes, still open (which) · yes, declined.

## Swap

You write one for their blog or newsletter; they write one for yours.

1. **Who's the partner, where do they publish, and how do you reach them?** Short answer: name, blog or newsletter, link, address.
2. **Who reads theirs, and where do their readers and yours overlap?** Short answer.
3. **What will you write for them, and what would you like from them?** Short answer: a topic each.
4. **Which formats?** blog post · newsletter issue · both, for each side.
5. **When does each piece go out?** Two dates, or "same week".
6. **What does each side get back?** a link to the author's site · a bio and headshot · a mention in social posts · a newsletter mention.
7. **What are their house rules for your piece?** Short answer: length, format, links, topics they take — or their guidelines' link.
8. **Do you have contributor guidelines to send them?** yes (link) · no, write them.

## Co-written

One piece written together.

1. **Who's the co-author, how do you reach them, and what do they bring?** Short answer.
2. **What's the piece, in one or two sentences?** Short answer.
3. **Who writes which part?** Short answer: sections or roles (one drafts, one edits).
4. **Whose platform publishes it first, and when?** yours · theirs; a date.
5. **Does the other platform take a full copy or an opening excerpt?** full copy · opening excerpt · neither.
6. **How do the bylines read?** both names, in which order · one name with "with".
7. **What do you need from them?** their data · an example · a review · nothing beyond their part.

## Guest on ours

Someone pitched you, or you're inviting them.

1. **Did they pitch you, or are you inviting them?** they pitched · inviting.
2. **Who are they, and what's the pitch or the topic you want?** Short answer, their address and links to their published work.
3. **Does it fit your readers and what you've already published?** yes · overlaps (with which piece) · no.
4. **Your answer?** accept · ask to revise (the change) · decline (the reason in a line).
5. **For an acceptance, what angle, length and draft date?** Short answer.
6. **How many rounds of edits, and what byline and links do they get?** A number; a bio length and the number of links.
7. **Do you have contributor guidelines to send?** yes (link) · no, write them.

## Sponsored

A brand appears in a slot in an issue, a whole issue or a sponsored post.

1. **Are you writing to the brand, or did they write to you?** pitching · they approached.
2. **Who's the brand, who's the contact, and why would your readers want the product?** Short answer: brand, name and address, product, the reader who'd want it.
3. **What would they appear in?** a slot in an issue · a whole issue · a sponsored post. Default: the options in `media-kit.md`.
4. **Who reads the publication?** Short answer. Default: `media-kit.md`, then `profile.md`.
5. **What are the current numbers, the date each was measured and where it came from?** subscribers · open rate · click rate · monthly readers. Default: `media-kit.md`, when its dates are within a month or two (common practice); never an estimate.
6. **Which dates are open?** Dates.
7. **What won't you say or do for any sponsor?** Default: "What it will never do" in `profile.md`.
